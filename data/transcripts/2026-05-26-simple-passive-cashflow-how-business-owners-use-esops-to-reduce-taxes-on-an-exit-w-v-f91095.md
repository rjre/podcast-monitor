summary: "CPA explains ESOPs (Employee Stock Ownership Plans) as a tax-advantaged way for business owners to sell part or all of a company to a trust for employees: sellers can get a full tax deduction on the transaction value, defer or eliminate capital-gains tax via a 1042 rollover into qualified securities, and -- because a leveraged ESOP transaction lowers the company's post-deal valuation -- unlock more efficient estate-gifting to heirs. Best suited to profitable, payroll-heavy, not-overly-leveraged businesses (engineering/professional services, staffing); poor fits include highly leveraged, low-payroll, or inventory-heavy businesses, or owners who want 100% of proceeds at closing."
