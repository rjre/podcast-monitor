How much emergency savings you need depends on your income, your debt, and your living expenses. But a wise rule of thumb is to keep at least three to six months worth of your living expenses in an FDIC-insured savings account. For instance, if you spend $3,000 a month on essentials, like your housing, food, health care, and debt, make a goal to keep at least three times that amount or $9,000 on hand.
