Financial manipulators use psychological abuse techniques similar to those commonly found in domestic violence. According to Cassandra Cross who did a fellowship on the topic of romance fraud with the Cybersecurity Cooperative Research Center. Offenders might prevent victims from communicating with family and friends, bombard them with messages to monopolize their attention or verbally abuse them to make them feel worthless. All of these tactics impair a victim's ability to think clearly about their situation or seek help. No one wakes up in the morning and decides to give away all their money.
