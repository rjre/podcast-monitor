Go where valuations are low, even if it's non-consensus. Here's how the portfolio is built. The fund methodology begins with a universe of about 45 countries located in both developed and emerging markets by using a composite of long-term valuation metrics like the 10-year, cyclically-adjusted price earnings ratio, but also measures based on dividends and cash flow too. It identifies the least expensive stock markets in the world. It then goes one step further and performs a bottom-up analysis using a valuation composite to select the most undervalued stocks out of the top 30 largest stocks within each country.
